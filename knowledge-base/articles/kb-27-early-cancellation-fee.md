# How the Early Cancellation Fee Is Calculated

**Article:** KB-27 · **Category:** Contracts & Cancellation

If you cancel your Blazz service before your 24-month contract ends, you'll pay an early cancellation fee. Here's how it's worked out.

## The rule

The fee is **$20 for each full month left** in your contract, counted from the day your service ends to your contract end date.

Only full months count. If you have 10 months and 2 weeks left, you pay for 10 months.

## How to work it out

1. Find your contract start date. It's on your first bill, or you can ask Blazz Support.
2. Add 24 months to find your contract end date.
3. Count the full months between the day your service ends and your contract end date.
4. Multiply by $20.

## Examples

- **Contract started March 10, 2025.** It ends March 10, 2027. Service ends October 15, 2026. From October 15, 2026 to March 10, 2027 is 4 full months. **Fee: 4 × $20 = $80.**
- **Contract started September 1, 2026.** It ends September 1, 2028. Service ends November 5, 2026. That leaves 21 full months. **Fee: 21 × $20 = $420.**
- **Contract started January 15, 2024.** It ended January 15, 2026, so the service is now month to month. **Fee: $0.**
- **Contract started September 20, 2026.** The customer asks to cancel on September 30, 2026 — within 15 days of the start date. **Fee: $0.**

The highest possible fee is **$460** (23 full months left).

## When there's no fee

- Your contract has already ended, and you're month to month.
- You ask to cancel within 15 days of your contract start date.

See "Your Blazz Contract and Early Cancellation: The Basics."

## Where the fee shows up

The early cancellation fee is added to your final bill. See "How to Cancel: From Request to Final Bill."

## What the fee doesn't include

The early cancellation fee is separate from:

- Your regular monthly charges, up to the day your service ends
- Fees for any equipment you don't return. See "Returning Your Equipment After You Cancel."

## Want to avoid the fee?

Switching to a lower-cost plan or pausing your service doesn't cost an early cancellation fee. See "Before You Cancel: Switching Plans or Pausing Your Service."
