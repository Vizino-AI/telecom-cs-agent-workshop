# When to Replace Equipment Instead of Troubleshooting

**Article:** KB-24 · **Category:** Diagnosis & Support Policy · **Applies to:** BlazzRouter R100, R200 · BlazzBox X150, X200 · BlazzMesh Extender

Most problems with your BlazzRouter or BlazzBox are fixed with a restart, a better setup, or a technician visit. But sometimes the device itself needs to be replaced or upgraded. Here's how to tell.

All Blazz equipment — routers, BlazzBoxes, BlazzMesh Extenders, and remotes — belongs to Blazz. You don't buy it. It's provided for as long as you have Blazz service.

## Replace it when it's broken

Blazz will send you a replacement, usually within 2 business days, if:

- **The device won't turn on** (no light at all), even after you've checked the power cable and tried a different outlet.
- **The device is physically damaged** — cracked, dropped, or wet — or it smells like burning or feels very hot. Unplug it right away.
- **The remote is faulty,** and still doesn't work with new batteries and after pairing again.

Replacements are free when a device stops working on its own. If a device was damaged by accident, like being dropped or having a drink spilled on it, a replacement fee applies: **$150 for a router, $100 for a BlazzBox, and $75 for a BlazzMesh Extender.**

## Don't ask for a replacement for a red light

A solid red light that stays red after a restart usually means a problem with the Blazz line, not the device. A new device won't fix that. Book a technician visit instead — see "When to Book a Technician Visit." The technician checks both the line and the device, and replaces the device on the spot if it's faulty.

## Upgrade it when it can't do what you need

Sometimes an older model works fine but can't do what you want. Then an upgrade makes more sense than more troubleshooting:

- **You want better Wi-Fi coverage with BlazzMesh Extenders, but you have a BlazzRouter R100.** Mesh only works with the BlazzRouter R200.
- **You want 4K TV, or your BlazzBox on Wi-Fi, but you have a BlazzBox X150.** Both need a BlazzBox X200.

Contact Blazz Support to ask about upgrading.

## Keep troubleshooting when

- **The light is solid green, but something doesn't work.** The device is fine. The problem is Wi-Fi, a setting, or another device.
- **Wi-Fi is weak in far rooms.** Try moving the router first.
- **The problem started right after a change,** like a new Wi-Fi password.

## Returning the old device

When you get a replacement or upgrade, send the old device back within 30 days, using the prepaid shipping label we send you.
