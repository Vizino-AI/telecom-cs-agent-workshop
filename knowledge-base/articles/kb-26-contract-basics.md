# Your Blazz Contract and Early Cancellation: The Basics

**Article:** KB-26 · **Category:** Contracts & Cancellation

This article explains how your Blazz contract works, and when you can cancel without a fee.

## Your contract term

- Blazz plans come with a **24-month contract**.
- Your contract starts on the day your service starts. This is your **contract start date**.
- Your contract ends 24 months later. This is your **contract end date**.

You can find your contract start date on your first bill, or ask Blazz Support.

## When your contract ends

When your 24 months are up, your service keeps going **month to month**. Your plan and price stay the same, and you can cancel any time with no early cancellation fee.

## Cancelling before your contract ends

If you cancel before your contract end date, an **early cancellation fee** applies. The fee is **$20 for each full month left** in your contract. See "How the Early Cancellation Fee Is Calculated" for examples.

## When there's no early cancellation fee

- Your contract has ended, and you're month to month.
- You ask to cancel **within 15 days** of your contract start date. This gives new customers time to try the service.

In both cases, you still need to return your equipment. See "Returning Your Equipment After You Cancel."

## What changes your contract end date

- **Switching plans doesn't.** If you switch to a different Blazz plan, your contract end date stays the same, and there's no fee.
- **Pausing your service does.** Your end date moves later by the number of months you pause.

See "Before You Cancel: Switching Plans or Pausing Your Service."

## Good to know before you cancel

- **You can't cancel in chat alone.** Cancelling always needs a signed Cancellation Request Form. See "How to Cancel: From Request to Final Bill."
- **If something isn't working, let us fix it first.** Technician visits are free when the problem is with the Blazz network or Blazz equipment. See "When to Book a Technician Visit."
