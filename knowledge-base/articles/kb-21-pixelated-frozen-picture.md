# Pixelated or Frozen TV Picture: Common Causes

**Article:** KB-21 · **Category:** BlazzBox & TV · **Applies to:** BlazzBox X150, X200

A **pixelated** picture looks blocky or blurry, as if it's made of little squares. A **frozen** picture stops moving, sometimes with the sound cutting out. Both usually mean your BlazzBox isn't getting a steady stream of TV data.

## First: one channel or all channels?

- **Only one channel:** The problem is probably with that channel's broadcast, not your equipment. These problems are usually temporary and fixed by Blazz. Try another channel, then try the first one again in 30 minutes. If it's still happening, contact Blazz Support and tell us which channel.
- **All channels:** The problem is likely your connection. Follow the steps below.

## Steps to fix it on all channels

1. **Check the router light.** If it's not solid green, fix the router first. See "What the Lights on Your BlazzRouter and BlazzBox Mean."
2. **Check the BlazzBox connection.**
   - **Connected with a cable (X150 or X200):** Make sure the Ethernet cable is firmly plugged in at both ends. Try a different Ethernet port on the router.
   - **BlazzBox X200 on Wi-Fi:** Wi-Fi is the most common cause of a pixelated picture. Move the router and box closer together if you can. Better still, connect them with an Ethernet cable.
3. **Check for heavy internet use at home.** Big downloads, game updates, or several people streaming at once leave less room for TV — especially when the box is on Wi-Fi. Pause big downloads and see if the picture improves.
4. **Restart the BlazzBox once.** See "How to Restart Your BlazzBox."

## If it's still pixelated or freezing

If the problem continues after a restart, and your box is connected with an Ethernet cable, contact Blazz Support. Tell us:

- Your BlazzBox model
- Whether the box uses a cable or Wi-Fi
- Whether it happens on all channels or just some
- What time of day it happens

## Not the same as a black screen

If your TV screen is completely black or shows "No Signal," see "HDMI Problems Between Your BlazzBox and TV." If it shows "Signal Lost" on a Blazz screen, see "'Signal Lost' Message on Your BlazzBox: What It Means."
