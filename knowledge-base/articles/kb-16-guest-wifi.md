# How to Set Up a Guest Wi-Fi Network

**Article:** KB-16 · **Category:** Equipment & Network Settings · **Applies to:** BlazzRouter R100, R200

A guest network is a separate Wi-Fi network for visitors. Your guests get internet access, but they can't see or reach the devices on your main network, like your computers, printers, or shared files. And you don't have to share your main Wi-Fi password.

Both the BlazzRouter R100 and BlazzRouter R200 support a guest network.

## How to turn it on

1. On a device connected to your home network, open a web browser and go to **192.168.1.1**.
2. Log in with your router admin password. It's printed on the router sticker, unless you changed it.
3. Select **Wi-Fi**, then **Guest Network**.
4. Turn Guest Network on.
5. Choose a name for it, like "Blazz-Guest," and a password of at least 8 characters.
6. Select **Save**.

Share the guest network's name and password with your visitors.

## How to turn it off

Go back to Wi-Fi, then Guest Network, and turn it off. Anyone connected to it will be disconnected.

## Good to know

- **Guests share your internet speed.** If several guests are streaming at once, your own devices may slow down.
- **The two passwords are separate.** Changing the guest password doesn't affect your main Wi-Fi, and changing your main password doesn't affect the guest network.
- **A factory reset turns the guest network off** and erases its name and password. See "Forgot Your Router Admin Password?"
- **Turn it off when you don't need it.** It's more secure.

## Using the guest network for smart home devices

Some people put smart plugs, cameras, or speakers on the guest network to keep them apart from their computers. That works fine. Just remember that those devices will disconnect too if you turn the guest network off.
