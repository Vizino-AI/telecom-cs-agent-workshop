# Before You Cancel: Switching Plans or Pausing Your Service

**Article:** KB-28 · **Category:** Contracts & Cancellation

If you're thinking about cancelling, there may be an option that suits you better — and costs less than an early cancellation fee. Here are the main alternatives.

## Switch to a different plan

If your plan costs more than you need, or you're not using all of it, you can switch to a lower-cost Blazz plan.

- There's no fee to switch.
- Your contract end date stays the same.
- The new plan starts at the beginning of your next billing month.

**How to switch:** Tell Blazz Support which plan you're interested in. A Blazz team member will review your request, contact you to confirm the details, and complete the change. Plan changes can't be completed directly in chat.

## Pause your service

If you're going away for a while — for example, traveling for the winter or working in another city — you can pause your service instead of cancelling.

- Pause for **1 to 6 months** at a time.
- You can pause **once every 12 months**.
- While your service is paused, you pay **$10 a month**, and your service is off.
- Your contract end date moves later by the number of months you pause. For example, a 3-month pause moves your end date 3 months later.
- Keep your equipment at home. It reconnects on its own when your service starts again.

**How to pause:** Contact Blazz Support at least 5 business days before you want the pause to start. A Blazz team member will confirm your dates by email.

## Fix the problem instead

If you're thinking about cancelling because something isn't working — like internet that keeps dropping, or a TV picture that freezes — let us try to fix it first. Many problems can be solved with a few simple steps, and technician visits are free when the problem is with the Blazz network or Blazz equipment. See "When to Book a Technician Visit."

## Compare the costs

If you have many months left in your contract, the early cancellation fee can be as high as $460. See "How the Early Cancellation Fee Is Calculated." Switching plans or pausing is often the cheaper choice.
