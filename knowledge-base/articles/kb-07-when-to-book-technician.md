# When to Book a Technician Visit

**Article:** KB-07 · **Category:** Status Lights & Troubleshooting · **Applies to:** BlazzRouter R100, R200 · BlazzBox X150, X200

Most connection problems can be fixed at home with a restart. But some problems can only be fixed by a Blazz technician. Here's how to tell the difference, and how booking works.

## Book a technician visit if any of these are true

1. Your router or BlazzBox light is **solid red**, and it's still red after one restart.
2. Your router or BlazzBox light is still **blinking amber 30 minutes after a restart**.
3. Your connection has **dropped 3 or more times in 7 days**, even if a restart fixed it each time.
4. You can see **damage to the Blazz line** — for example, a cut or crushed cable, or a loose or broken wall jack.

## A technician can't help with

- Problems with your own devices, like a phone, laptop, game console, or smart TV
- A forgotten Wi-Fi password or router admin password. See "Forgot Your Router Admin Password?"
- Wi-Fi settings, guest networks, or parental controls
- Weak Wi-Fi in far rooms when your wired speed is normal. See "Weak Wi-Fi in Some Rooms: BlazzMesh Extenders."

## How booking works

1. Contact Blazz Support and describe the problem. Tell us what you've already tried, like restarting.
2. We'll look up the technicians available in your area and offer you a few time slots. Visits happen on weekdays (Monday to Friday), in one of two time windows: **9 a.m. to 11 a.m.** or **1 p.m. to 3 p.m.**
3. Pick the slot that works for you.
4. A Blazz team member reviews and confirms every booking. Once it's confirmed, you'll get a confirmation email with your technician's name, the date, and the time window.
5. If your slot can't be confirmed, we'll let you know and offer you another available time.

## On the day of the visit

- An adult (18 or older) must be home.
- Leave your router and BlazzBox plugged in, and don't do a factory reset.
- Most visits take less than an hour.

## Cost

Technician visits are free when the problem is with the Blazz network or Blazz equipment.
