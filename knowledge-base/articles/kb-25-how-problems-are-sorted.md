# How Blazz Sorts Technical Problems: Internet, TV, or Equipment

**Article:** KB-25 · **Category:** Diagnosis & Support Policy · **Applies to:** BlazzRouter R100, R200 · BlazzBox X150, X200 · BlazzMesh Extender

When you contact Blazz Support with a technical problem, the first thing we do is work out which kind of problem it is. Each kind has different causes and different fixes. You can use the same approach yourself.

## 1. Internet problems

**Signs:**
- No internet on any device
- Slow speeds
- Wi-Fi that drops, or doesn't reach some rooms

**Where to look first:** the light on your BlazzRouter.

**Common fixes:** restarting the router, moving it to a better spot, using an Ethernet cable, or adding BlazzMesh Extenders.

**A technician is needed if** the router light stays solid red after a restart, is still blinking amber 30 minutes after a restart, or the connection drops 3 or more times in 7 days.

## 2. TV problems

**Signs:**
- A "Signal Lost" message on a Blazz screen
- A pixelated or frozen picture
- A black screen, or "No Signal" from your TV
- A remote that doesn't work

**Where to look first:** the router light, then the BlazzBox light. Your BlazzBox gets TV through the router, so if the internet is down, TV is down too. If the internet works but TV doesn't, the problem is between the box and the router, in the box itself, or in the HDMI connection to your TV.

**Common fixes:** checking cables, choosing the right input on your TV, restarting the BlazzBox, or pairing the remote again.

**A technician is needed if** the router light is solid green, but the BlazzBox light stays solid red after a restart, or is still blinking amber 30 minutes after a restart.

## 3. Equipment problems

**Signs:**
- No light at all, even with a working outlet
- Physical damage — cracked, dropped, wet, a burning smell, or very hot

**Where to look first:** the device itself. Unplug a damaged device right away.

**Common fix:** Blazz sends a replacement device. See "When to Replace Equipment Instead of Troubleshooting."

## Not a technical problem?

Questions about your bill, changing your plan, your contract, or cancelling are account questions, not technical ones. Blazz Support handles those too — just tell us what you need.
