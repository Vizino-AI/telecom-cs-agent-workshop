# Software (Firmware) Updates: Why They Matter and How They Work

**Article:** KB-18 · **Category:** Equipment & Network Settings · **Applies to:** BlazzRouter R100, R200 · BlazzBox X150, X200

Firmware is the software built into your BlazzRouter and BlazzBox. Blazz releases firmware updates to fix problems, improve security, and keep your devices running smoothly.

## Why updates matter

- **Security:** Updates close gaps that could let others get into your network.
- **Stability:** Updates fix bugs that can cause dropped connections or a frozen screen.
- **Performance:** Some updates improve Wi-Fi speed or range.

## Updates happen automatically

You don't need to do anything. Your BlazzRouter and BlazzBox check for updates every night and install them between 2 a.m. and 5 a.m., when you're least likely to be using them. If your internet or TV cuts out for a few minutes in the middle of the night, that's usually an update being installed.

## How to tell an update is happening

The device's light **blinks green**. An update takes up to 10 minutes. Don't unplug the device while the light is blinking green. If it's still blinking green after 30 minutes, contact Blazz Support.

## If you unplugged it during an update

Plug it back in. The device will restart and try the update again. When the light turns solid green, it's done.

## Check for updates yourself (optional)

- **BlazzRouter:** On a device connected to your home network, go to 192.168.1.1 and log in with your router admin password. Then select System, then Firmware Update, then Check for Updates.
- **BlazzBox:** On your TV, go to Settings, then System, then Software Update.

If an update is available, the device installs it and restarts. You'll see the blinking green light while it installs.

## Good to know

- **Updates don't change your settings.** Your Wi-Fi name, passwords, guest network, and parental controls stay the same.
- **Older models still get security updates.** The BlazzRouter R100 and BlazzBox X150 keep receiving security updates, but some new features are only available on the BlazzRouter R200 and BlazzBox X200.
