# Weak Wi-Fi in Some Rooms: BlazzMesh Extenders

**Article:** KB-10 · **Category:** Internet Connection & Speed · **Applies to:** BlazzRouter R200 · BlazzMesh Extender

In a large home, a home with several floors, or one with thick walls, a single router may not reach every room. A BlazzMesh Extender fixes this by passing your Wi-Fi signal along to the rooms that need it.

## How mesh works

A BlazzMesh Extender is a small device that plugs into a wall outlet. It connects to your BlazzRouter without a cable and creates a strong Wi-Fi signal around it. Your devices move between the router and the extenders automatically, using the same Wi-Fi name and password. You don't have to switch networks as you walk around your home.

## Which routers work with BlazzMesh

- **BlazzRouter R200:** Yes. You can add up to 3 BlazzMesh Extenders.
- **BlazzRouter R100:** No. The R100 doesn't support mesh. To use BlazzMesh Extenders, you'd first need to upgrade to a BlazzRouter R200. Contact Blazz Support to ask about upgrading.

## Before you add an extender

Make sure the problem really is range. Try moving your router to a more central spot first. See "Wi-Fi Keeps Cutting In and Out: Check Where Your Router Is." If Wi-Fi is weak even close to the router, an extender won't help — contact Blazz Support.

## Where to put an extender

- About halfway between your router and the room with weak Wi-Fi
- Not in the weak room itself — the extender needs a good signal from the router to pass along
- Out in the open, not behind furniture or inside a cabinet

## Check the extender's light

- **Solid green:** Good connection to your router.
- **Blinking amber:** The extender is trying to connect, but the router's signal is too weak where it is. Move it closer to the router.

## Setting it up

1. Plug the extender into an outlet in the right spot.
2. Follow the setup card that comes in the box. It takes about 5 minutes.
3. Wait for the extender's light to turn solid green.

## How to get one

Contact Blazz Support to add BlazzMesh Extenders to your account. Like your router and BlazzBox, BlazzMesh Extenders are Blazz equipment.
