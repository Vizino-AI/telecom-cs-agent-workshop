# Three Things to Check Before You Start Troubleshooting

**Article:** KB-23 · **Category:** Diagnosis & Support Policy · **Applies to:** BlazzRouter R100, R200 · BlazzBox X150, X200

Before you restart anything or contact Blazz Support, take two minutes to check these three things. They tell you where the problem is, and they often point straight to the fix.

## 1. Is it one device or all devices?

Try getting online with a second device — for example, a phone and a laptop.

- **Only one device has a problem:** The problem is that device, not your Blazz service. Restart it, or forget the Wi-Fi network on it and join again. See "Mobile Data Works but Home Wi-Fi Doesn't: What's Wrong?"
- **All devices have a problem:** The problem is your home connection. Keep going.

If the problem is only with your TV, check your internet on a phone or laptop too. If the internet works but the TV doesn't, it's a BlazzBox problem. See "How Blazz Sorts Technical Problems: Internet, TV, or Equipment."

## 2. What does the router light show?

Look at the light on the front of your BlazzRouter.

- **Solid green:** Your connection to the Blazz network is working. The problem is likely Wi-Fi or a device.
- **Blinking green:** An update is installing. Wait up to 10 minutes.
- **Blinking amber:** The router is trying to connect.
- **Solid red:** No signal from the Blazz network.
- **No light:** No power.

For what to do in each case, see "What the Lights on Your BlazzRouter and BlazzBox Mean."

## 3. Are the power and cables OK?

- Is the router's power cable firmly plugged into the router and into a working outlet?
- Is the Blazz line firmly connected at the wall and at the router?
- If you have a BlazzBox, are its HDMI cable and Ethernet cable (if it uses one) firmly connected?
- Has anyone recently moved the router or unplugged something?

## Keep track of what you've tried

Before you contact Blazz Support, note down:

- The light color, and whether it's solid or blinking
- Whether you've restarted, and how many times
- When the problem started

Telling us this up front means you won't be asked to repeat steps you've already tried. For example, if you've already restarted your router and the light is still solid red, we'll skip straight to booking a technician.
