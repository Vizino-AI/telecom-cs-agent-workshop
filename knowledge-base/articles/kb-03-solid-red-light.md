# Solid Red Light: Why It Can't Be Fixed at Home

**Article:** KB-03 · **Category:** Status Lights & Troubleshooting · **Applies to:** BlazzRouter R100, R200 · BlazzBox X150, X200

A solid red light on your BlazzRouter or BlazzBox means the device has no signal from the Blazz network. Unlike a blinking amber light, a solid red light doesn't mean "working on it." It means the connection is down.

## Why it happens

A solid red light is usually caused by one of these:

- **A problem with the Blazz line** — for example, a damaged cable outside your home, a problem at the wall jack, or a fault in the network equipment in your area.
- **A hardware fault** inside the device itself.

Both need a trained technician with special tools. There are no settings you can change and no buttons you can press at home that will fix them.

## What to do

1. Restart the device once. Once in a while, a solid red light is caused by a short glitch that a restart clears. See "How to Restart Your BlazzRouter" or "How to Restart Your BlazzBox."
2. Wait up to 5 minutes after the restart.
3. If the light is still solid red, stop troubleshooting and contact Blazz Support to book a technician visit. Technician visits for this kind of problem are free.

## If you've already restarted

If you've already restarted the device at least once and the light is still red, you don't need to restart it again. Restarting again and again won't fix a line or hardware problem. Just contact Blazz Support and tell us you've already restarted — we'll go straight to booking a technician.

## What not to do

- **Don't press the Reset pinhole button.** A factory reset erases your settings and can't fix a line or hardware problem.
- **Don't unplug the Blazz line from the wall** or try to repair the wall jack yourself.
- **Don't ask for a replacement device first.** A solid red light is usually a line problem, not a device problem. The technician checks both, and replaces the device on the spot if it's faulty.

## If only the BlazzBox is red

If your router is solid green but your BlazzBox is solid red, check the Ethernet cable between them first. For a BlazzBox X200 on Wi-Fi, check how far it is from the router. If the box is still red after that and one restart, contact Blazz Support to book a technician visit.
