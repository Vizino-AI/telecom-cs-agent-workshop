# How to Restart Your BlazzRouter

**Article:** KB-05 · **Category:** Status Lights & Troubleshooting · **Applies to:** BlazzRouter R100, R200

Restarting your BlazzRouter (also called rebooting or power cycling) is the first thing to try when your internet stops working, keeps dropping, or feels unusually slow. It clears small glitches and doesn't change any of your settings.

## Know your cables

The back of your router has:

- A **power cable**, which goes to the outlet
- The **Blazz line**, the cable that comes from the wall
- **Four Ethernet ports** for wired devices, like a BlazzBox or a computer

For a restart, you only touch the power cable.

## How to restart your BlazzRouter

1. Unplug the power cable from the back of the router. Don't unplug the Blazz line.
2. Wait 30 seconds.
3. Plug the power cable back in.
4. Wait up to 5 minutes. The light will blink amber while the router reconnects.
5. When the light turns solid green, your internet is back. Your Wi-Fi devices will reconnect on their own.

## If your TV isn't working either

Restart the router first and wait until its light is solid green. Then restart your BlazzBox. See "How to Restart Your BlazzBox."

## What to expect after the restart

- **Solid green:** All good.
- **Blinking amber:** Still connecting. See "Blinking Amber Light: Reconnecting vs. Disconnected."
- **Solid red:** See "Solid Red Light: Why It Can't Be Fixed at Home."

## Restart once, not over and over

One restart is enough. If the problem doesn't go away after one restart, restarting again won't fix it. Go to "The Light Is Still Wrong After a Restart: What to Do Next."

If your connection keeps dropping and you've had to restart 3 or more times in 7 days, contact Blazz Support. That's a sign of a problem that needs a technician, even if each restart seems to fix it for a while.

## Don't use the Reset button

The small pinhole button labeled Reset is for a factory reset, not a restart. It erases your Wi-Fi name, your Wi-Fi password, and all your other settings. Only use it if you've forgotten your router admin password. See "Forgot Your Router Admin Password?"
