# How to Restart Your BlazzBox

**Article:** KB-04 · **Category:** Status Lights & Troubleshooting · **Applies to:** BlazzBox X150, X200

Restarting your BlazzBox (also called rebooting or power cycling) clears small glitches, like a frozen screen or a menu that won't respond. It doesn't erase any of your settings.

## Before you start

Look at your BlazzRouter's light first. If the router isn't solid green, restart the router before the BlazzBox. The box can't connect until the router is working. See "How to Restart Your BlazzRouter."

## How to restart your BlazzBox

1. Unplug the power cable from the back of the BlazzBox. Leave the other cables (HDMI and Ethernet) plugged in.
2. Wait 30 seconds.
3. Plug the power cable back in.
4. Wait up to 5 minutes. You'll see the Blazz logo on your TV while the box starts up.
5. When the box's light is solid green and your TV shows the Blazz home screen, it's ready.

## What to expect after the restart

- **Solid green:** All good. Try whatever wasn't working before.
- **Blinking amber:** The box is still connecting. See "Blinking Amber Light: Reconnecting vs. Disconnected."
- **Solid red:** The box can't reach the Blazz network. See "Solid Red Light: Why It Can't Be Fixed at Home."

## Restart once, not over and over

One restart is enough. If the problem is still there after one restart, restarting again won't help. Go to "The Light Is Still Wrong After a Restart: What to Do Next" instead.

## Restart vs. factory reset

A restart is not the same as a factory reset. A restart only turns the box off and on again. A factory reset erases all your settings. Only do a factory reset on your BlazzBox if Blazz Support asks you to, and never use one to fix a connection problem.
