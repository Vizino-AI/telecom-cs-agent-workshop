# How to Cancel: From Request to Final Bill

**Article:** KB-30 · **Category:** Contracts & Cancellation

Here's what happens when you cancel your Blazz service, step by step.

## Before you start

Check whether an early cancellation fee applies — see "How the Early Cancellation Fee Is Calculated." And see whether switching plans or pausing might suit you better — see "Before You Cancel: Switching Plans or Pausing Your Service."

## Step 1: Ask to cancel

Tell Blazz Support that you want to cancel. Blazz Support can't cancel your service directly in chat. Cancelling always needs your signed form.

## Step 2: Get your Cancellation Request Form

We'll email you a Cancellation Request Form.

## Step 3: Fill it in and send it back

Complete the form, sign it, and email it back to us. Your cancellation isn't final until we receive your signed form.

## Step 4: Get your confirmation

Within 1 business day of receiving your form, we'll email you a confirmation. It includes the date your service ends and a prepaid shipping label for your equipment.

Your service ends **2 business days after we receive your signed form.**

## Step 5: Return your equipment

Send back your equipment within 30 days after your service ends. See "Returning Your Equipment After You Cancel."

## Step 6: Your final bill

Your final bill arrives about **45 days after your service ends**, once the 30-day return window has closed. It includes:

- **A credit for unused days.** Monthly charges are billed in advance, so if your service ends partway through a month, you get a credit for the days you didn't use.
- **Your early cancellation fee,** if one applies
- **Fees for any equipment we haven't received**

If your final bill shows a credit — meaning Blazz owes you money — we'll refund it to your payment method within 30 days.

## About refunds

Blazz doesn't refund charges for service you've already used. The only refund is the credit for unused days in your last month, when your final bill shows that Blazz owes you money.

## Changed your mind?

If you change your mind before your service end date, contact Blazz Support and we'll stop the cancellation. There's no fee.
