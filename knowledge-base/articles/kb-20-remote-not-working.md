# TV Remote Not Working or Won't Pair With Your BlazzBox

**Article:** KB-20 · **Category:** BlazzBox & TV · **Applies to:** BlazzBox X150, X200

Your BlazzBox model decides which kind of remote you have. You'll find the model name on the label on the bottom of the box.

## BlazzBox X150: infrared remote

The X150 remote works like a classic TV remote. It sends an invisible signal straight from the front of the remote to the front of the box. It doesn't need pairing.

If it's not working:

1. Replace the batteries (2 AA).
2. Point the remote directly at the front of the BlazzBox.
3. Make sure nothing is blocking the path between the remote and the box, like a cabinet door, a soundbar, or decorations.
4. Stand within 5 meters (about 16 feet) of the box.
5. Restart the BlazzBox once. See "How to Restart Your BlazzBox."

## BlazzBox X200: Bluetooth voice remote

The X200 remote connects to the box using Bluetooth, so it doesn't need to point at the box. It has a microphone button for voice search. It must be **paired** with your box to work. Pairing is done when your BlazzBox is installed, but sometimes the remote needs to be paired again.

How to pair (or re-pair) the X200 remote:

1. Make sure the BlazzBox is on and your TV shows the Blazz home screen.
2. Stand within 3 meters (about 10 feet) of the box.
3. Press and hold the **Home** and **Back** buttons together for 5 seconds.
4. The light on the remote blinks. When pairing is done, your TV shows "Remote paired."

If it won't pair:

1. Replace the batteries (2 AA) and try again.
2. Restart the BlazzBox, then try pairing again.
3. Move away from other Bluetooth devices, like speakers or headphones, while you pair.

## If the remote still doesn't work

Contact Blazz Support. If your remote is faulty, we'll send you a replacement free of charge.
