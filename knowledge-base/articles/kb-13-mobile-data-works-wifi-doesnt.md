# Mobile Data Works but Home Wi-Fi Doesn't: What's Wrong?

**Article:** KB-13 · **Category:** Internet Connection & Speed · **Applies to:** BlazzRouter R100, R200

If your phone can get online using mobile data but not on your home Wi-Fi, the problem is somewhere between your phone and your home internet. Here's how to find it.

## Step 1: Check the router light

- **Solid green:** Your home internet is working. The problem is likely your phone's Wi-Fi connection. Go to Step 2.
- **Blinking amber, solid red, or no light:** Your home internet is down, so your phone's Wi-Fi has nothing to connect to — even if your phone says it's connected. See "What the Lights on Your BlazzRouter and BlazzBox Mean."

## Step 2: Check other devices

Try getting online with another device on your home Wi-Fi, like a laptop or tablet.

- **Other devices work:** The problem is your phone. Go to Step 3.
- **No devices work:** Restart your router once. See "How to Restart Your BlazzRouter."

## Step 3: Fix your phone's Wi-Fi connection

Try these one at a time:

1. **Make sure you're on your own network.** Your network name starts with "Blazz-" unless you changed it. Your phone may have joined a neighbor's network or a public hotspot instead.
2. **Turn Wi-Fi off and on.** Turn off Wi-Fi on your phone, wait 10 seconds, and turn it back on.
3. **Forget the network and join again.** In your phone's Wi-Fi settings, choose your network, tap "Forget," then join again with your Wi-Fi password.
4. **Restart your phone.**

## Common causes

- **The Wi-Fi password was changed.** If someone changed the password on the router, every device needs the new one. See "How to Change Your Wi-Fi Name and Password."
- **Too far from the router.** On a BlazzRouter R100, the network ending in "-5G" doesn't reach as far. Try the network without "-5G."
- **Parental controls.** If your phone has a parental control schedule, it can't get online during the blocked hours. See "How to Use Parental Controls to Limit Internet Time."
- **Battery saver or airplane mode.** Some phones turn off Wi-Fi to save battery.

## Where to find your Wi-Fi password

If you've never changed it, your Wi-Fi password is printed on the sticker on the bottom of your router.
