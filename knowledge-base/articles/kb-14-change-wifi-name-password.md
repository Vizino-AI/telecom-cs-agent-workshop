# How to Change Your Wi-Fi Name and Password

**Article:** KB-14 · **Category:** Equipment & Network Settings · **Applies to:** BlazzRouter R100, R200

You can change your Wi-Fi network name and password at any time from your router's settings page.

## What you'll need

- A phone, tablet, or computer connected to your home network
- Your **router admin password**. If you've never changed it, it's printed on the sticker on the bottom of your router, labeled "Admin Password." It's different from your Wi-Fi password, which is on the same sticker.

## How to change them

1. Open a web browser and go to **192.168.1.1**.
2. Log in with your router admin password.
3. Select **Wi-Fi**.
4. Type your new network name and new password.
5. Select **Save**. The router applies the changes in about a minute.

## Tips for a good password

- At least 8 characters (required)
- A mix of words and numbers that's easy for you to remember but hard for others to guess
- Don't use your name, address, or phone number

## On the BlazzRouter R100

The R100 has two networks: one for 2.4 GHz, and one for 5 GHz (the one ending in "-5G"). You'll see both on the Wi-Fi page. You can give them the same password to keep things simple. The BlazzRouter R200 has just one network name, so there's only one to change.

## After you change your password

Every device on your Wi-Fi will disconnect. Reconnect each one using the new name and password:

- **Phones, tablets, and laptops:** Choose the new network name and type the new password.
- **Smart TVs, speakers, and printers:** Check each device's Wi-Fi settings.
- **BlazzBox X200 on Wi-Fi:** On your TV, go to Settings, then Network, then Wi-Fi, and join with the new password.
- **BlazzBox X150:** No change needed. It uses an Ethernet cable, not Wi-Fi.
- **BlazzMesh Extenders:** No change needed. They get the new details from your router automatically.

## If you forget the new password

If you can still log in to the settings page, you can look up your Wi-Fi password on the Wi-Fi page. If you've also forgotten your admin password, see "Forgot Your Router Admin Password?"
