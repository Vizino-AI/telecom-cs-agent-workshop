# Is Slower Internet in the Evening Normal?

**Article:** KB-12 · **Category:** Internet Connection & Speed · **Applies to:** BlazzRouter R100, R200

A little, yes. Many people find their internet a bit slower in the evening. Here's why, and how to tell a normal slowdown from a real problem.

## Why evenings are busier

Between about 7 p.m. and 11 p.m., most people are home and online at the same time — streaming shows, gaming, and video calling. These are called **peak hours**. The Blazz network in your area is shared by many homes, so when everyone is online at once, speeds can dip a little.

Your own home gets busier too. In the evening, several people in your household may be streaming or gaming at the same time, all sharing one connection.

## What's normal

- **Outside peak hours,** a wired speed test should show at least 80% of your plan speed.
- **During peak hours,** a wired speed test may dip lower, but it should stay above 50% of your plan speed.

## What's not normal

- A wired speed test below 50% of your plan speed, on more than one test
- Slow speeds all day, not just in the evening
- The connection dropping completely, with the router light turning amber or red

To check, run a speed test on a computer connected to your router with an Ethernet cable. See "Slow Internet: Common Causes and What to Check."

## How to make evenings smoother

- Schedule big downloads, like game updates or phone backups, for overnight or during the day.
- Use an Ethernet cable for your BlazzBox, game console, or desktop computer.
- Lower the video quality on screens that don't need it, like a phone in the kitchen.
- Pause downloads on devices nobody is using.

## When to contact us

If wired speed tests are below 50% of your plan speed, restart your router once. If speeds are still that low after the restart, contact Blazz Support. Tell us your speed test results and the times you tested.
