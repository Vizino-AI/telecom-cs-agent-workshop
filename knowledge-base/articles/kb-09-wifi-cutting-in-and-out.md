# Wi-Fi Keeps Cutting In and Out: Check Where Your Router Is

**Article:** KB-09 · **Category:** Internet Connection & Speed · **Applies to:** BlazzRouter R100, R200

If your Wi-Fi keeps dropping but your router light stays solid green the whole time, the problem is usually where your router is placed. Wi-Fi signals get weaker with distance, and when they pass through walls, floors, and certain objects.

## First, check the light

If the router light turns amber or red when your internet drops, the problem is the connection to the Blazz network, not your Wi-Fi. See "What the Lights on Your BlazzRouter and BlazzBox Mean."

## Where to put your router

- In the middle of your home, not in a corner or at one end
- Up high, like on a shelf or table — not on the floor
- Out in the open, not inside a cabinet, closet, or TV stand
- Standing upright, with nothing stacked on top of it

## What to keep it away from

- Microwaves (Wi-Fi often drops while a microwave is running)
- Cordless phone bases and baby monitors
- Thick walls, especially concrete or brick
- Large mirrors, metal furniture, and fish tanks

## Other things that cause drops

- **Too many walls or floors** between you and the router. Each one weakens the signal.
- **Being at the edge of the Wi-Fi range.** On a BlazzRouter R100, the network ending in "-5G" drops sooner as you move away. In far rooms, switch to the network without "-5G." The BlazzRouter R200 does this for you.
- **A device in battery-saving mode.** Some phones and laptops disconnect from Wi-Fi on their own to save power. That's the device, not your Wi-Fi.

## A quick test

Stand in the same room as the router and use your device for 10 minutes. If Wi-Fi works fine there but drops in other rooms, it's a range problem. Moving the router to a more central spot often fixes it.

## Can't move the router?

The router has to stay connected to the Blazz line from the wall. If the cable doesn't reach a better spot, don't pull or extend it yourself. A BlazzMesh Extender is usually the easiest fix. See "Weak Wi-Fi in Some Rooms: BlazzMesh Extenders."

## Drops even right next to the router

Restart your router once. See "How to Restart Your BlazzRouter." If Wi-Fi keeps dropping even when you're close to the router, contact Blazz Support.
