# Why a Wired Connection Is More Stable Than Wi-Fi

**Article:** KB-11 · **Category:** Internet Connection & Speed · **Applies to:** BlazzRouter R100, R200 · BlazzBox X150, X200

A wired connection means plugging a device straight into your BlazzRouter with an Ethernet cable. It's usually faster and more stable than Wi-Fi. Here's why, and when it's worth using.

## Why wired is better

- **No walls in the way.** Wi-Fi signals get weaker through walls and floors. A cable doesn't.
- **No interference.** Microwaves, cordless phones, and your neighbors' Wi-Fi can disturb a Wi-Fi signal. A cable isn't affected.
- **No sharing the air.** On Wi-Fi, all your wireless devices share the same signal. A wired device gets its own path to the router.
- **More speed.** A wired connection usually gets at least 80% of your plan speed. Wi-Fi is often lower, depending on distance and walls.

## When to use a cable

Use a cable for devices that stay in one place and need a steady connection:

- Your BlazzBox. A cable is required for the BlazzBox X150 and recommended for the BlazzBox X200.
- Desktop computers
- Game consoles
- Smart TVs near the router
- A computer you use for video calls

Wi-Fi is fine for phones, tablets, and laptops that move around the house.

## How to connect with a cable

1. Plug one end of an Ethernet cable into one of the four Ethernet ports on the back of your BlazzRouter.
2. Plug the other end into your device.
3. Most devices connect automatically. You don't need a Wi-Fi password.

## Use a cable for speed tests

To find your true internet speed, always test on a device connected by cable. A Wi-Fi test mixes up two things: the speed of your Blazz connection and the quality of your Wi-Fi. See "Slow Internet: Common Causes and What to Check."

## A cable can't fix a line problem

If your router light is blinking amber or solid red, plugging in a cable won't help. The problem is between the router and the Blazz network, not between the router and your device. See "What the Lights on Your BlazzRouter and BlazzBox Mean."
