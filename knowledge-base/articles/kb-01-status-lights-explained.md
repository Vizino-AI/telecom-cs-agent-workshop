# What the Lights on Your BlazzRouter and BlazzBox Mean

**Article:** KB-01 · **Category:** Status Lights & Troubleshooting · **Applies to:** BlazzRouter R100, R200 · BlazzBox X150, X200

Your BlazzRouter and your BlazzBox each have one status light on the front. It's the quickest way to tell what's going on. Both devices use the same colors, and each color means the same thing on every model.

## How your equipment connects

Your BlazzRouter connects your home to the Blazz network through the Blazz line — the cable that comes from the wall. Everything else in your home gets its connection through the router: your phones, your laptops, and your BlazzBox. That's why, when the router has a problem, your TV usually stops working too.

## What each light means

- **Solid green:** Working normally. The device is connected to the Blazz network. If something still isn't working while the light is solid green, the cause is usually your Wi-Fi or one of your own devices, not the Blazz line.
- **Blinking green:** Installing a software update. This takes up to 10 minutes. Don't unplug the device while it's blinking green.
- **Blinking amber:** Trying to connect to the Blazz network. This is normal for up to 5 minutes after the device is turned on or restarted. If it keeps blinking amber for more than 10 minutes, restart the device once.
- **Solid red:** No signal from the Blazz network. There's a problem with the line or with the device itself. You can restart once to rule out a short glitch, but if the light is still red after a restart, it can't be fixed at home and a technician visit is needed.
- **No light:** The device has no power. Check that the power cable is firmly plugged into the device and into a working outlet.

## Always check the router first

If something isn't working, look at the router's light before the BlazzBox's light. If the router isn't solid green, fix the router first. The BlazzBox can't work until the router does.

## Tell us what you see

When you contact Blazz Support, tell us the color of the light, whether it's solid or blinking, and whether you've already restarted the device. That way, you won't be asked to repeat steps you've already tried.
