# HDMI Problems Between Your BlazzBox and TV

**Article:** KB-22 · **Category:** BlazzBox & TV · **Applies to:** BlazzBox X150, X200

Your BlazzBox sends picture and sound to your TV through an HDMI cable. If that connection has a problem, you might see a black screen, a "No Signal" message from your TV, a flickering picture, or no sound.

## Tell the problem apart

- **"No Signal" from your TV itself:** The TV isn't receiving anything from the box. Follow the steps below.
- **"Signal Lost" on a Blazz screen:** The HDMI connection is working, but the box can't reach Blazz TV. See "'Signal Lost' Message on Your BlazzBox: What It Means."

## Steps to fix it

1. **Check the BlazzBox is on.** Its light should be on, in any color. If there's no light, check the power cable and outlet.
2. **Choose the right input on your TV.** Press the Input or Source button on your TV's own remote (not the BlazzBox remote). Pick the HDMI port the BlazzBox is plugged into, like HDMI 1 or HDMI 2.
3. **Check the HDMI cable.** Unplug it from both the box and the TV, then plug it back in firmly.
4. **Try a different HDMI port** on your TV, then choose that port with the Input button.
5. **Try a different HDMI cable,** ideally the one that came with your BlazzBox.
6. **Restart the BlazzBox once.** See "How to Restart Your BlazzBox."

## Picture quality and your TV

- **BlazzBox X150:** Shows HD, up to 1080p. It works with almost any TV that has an HDMI port.
- **BlazzBox X200:** Shows up to 4K. If your TV doesn't support 4K, the X200 automatically switches to 1080p.

If the X200 picture flickers or goes black for a second now and then, use the HDMI cable that came with the box. Plug it into an HDMI port on your TV labeled 4K or HDR, if your TV has one. Older or very long HDMI cables may not handle 4K.

## No sound, but the picture is fine

- Check that your TV isn't muted and the volume is up.
- If you use a soundbar, check that it's on and set to the right input.

## If none of this works

Try connecting the BlazzBox to a different TV, if you can. If it works on the other TV, the problem is with the first TV or its HDMI port. If it doesn't work on any TV, contact Blazz Support.
