# Returning Your Equipment After You Cancel

**Article:** KB-29 · **Category:** Contracts & Cancellation · **Applies to:** BlazzRouter R100, R200 · BlazzBox X150, X200 · BlazzMesh Extender

All Blazz equipment — routers, BlazzBoxes, BlazzMesh Extenders, and remotes — belongs to Blazz. When your service ends, you need to send it back.

## What to return

- Your BlazzRouter (R100 or R200), with its power cable
- Your BlazzBox (X150 or X200), with its remote and power cable
- Any BlazzMesh Extenders

You can keep the Ethernet and HDMI cables.

## How to return it

1. When your cancellation is confirmed, the confirmation email includes a **prepaid shipping label**.
2. Pack the equipment in any sturdy box. You don't need the original packaging. Wrap each device so it doesn't get damaged in the mail.
3. Attach the label, and drop the box off at any post office.
4. Keep your receipt with the tracking number until your final bill arrives.

You don't need to reset the router or erase your settings first. Blazz erases all settings from returned equipment.

## The deadline

Return your equipment **within 30 days** after your service ends.

## If you don't return it

If we haven't received your equipment 30 days after your service ends, a fee for each missing item is added to your final bill:

- **BlazzRouter:** $150
- **BlazzBox:** $100
- **BlazzMesh Extender:** $75

## Returning equipment when you're not cancelling

The same steps apply when you get a replacement or an upgrade. We'll send you a prepaid shipping label, and you return the old device within 30 days. See "When to Replace Equipment Instead of Troubleshooting."

## Cancelling within 15 days?

Even if you cancel within 15 days of your contract start date and don't pay an early cancellation fee, you still need to return your equipment within 30 days after your service ends.
