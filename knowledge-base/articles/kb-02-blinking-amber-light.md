# Blinking Amber Light: Reconnecting vs. Disconnected

**Article:** KB-02 · **Category:** Status Lights & Troubleshooting · **Applies to:** BlazzRouter R100, R200 · BlazzBox X150, X200

A blinking amber light means your device is trying to connect to the Blazz network but hasn't finished yet. It's not the same as a solid red light, and the difference matters.

## Reconnecting vs. disconnected

- **Blinking amber = reconnecting.** The device is working on connecting to the Blazz network. This often fixes itself.
- **Solid red = disconnected.** The device can't find any signal from the Blazz network. This doesn't fix itself. See "Solid Red Light: Why It Can't Be Fixed at Home."

## When blinking amber is normal

- For up to 5 minutes after you turn on or restart the device
- Right after a power outage in your home, while the device starts up again
- During short maintenance work on the Blazz network, which usually happens overnight

## What to do

1. Wait 10 minutes. Many amber lights turn solid green on their own.
2. If it's still blinking amber after 10 minutes, restart the device once. See "How to Restart Your BlazzRouter" or "How to Restart Your BlazzBox."
3. After the restart, the light should turn solid green within 5 minutes. If it's still blinking amber, keep waiting — some network problems clear up on their own.
4. If it's still blinking amber 30 minutes after the restart, contact Blazz Support. The problem is most likely on the network side, and a technician visit may be needed.

## What not to do

- **Don't restart over and over.** One restart is enough. Each restart makes the device start connecting all over again, so repeated restarts only slow things down.
- **Don't press the Reset pinhole button.** That's a factory reset. It erases your Wi-Fi name, password, and other settings, and it won't fix the connection.

## If only the BlazzBox is blinking amber

If your router is solid green but your BlazzBox is blinking amber, the box is having trouble reaching the router. Check that its Ethernet cable is firmly plugged in at both ends. If you have a BlazzBox X200 on Wi-Fi, it may be too far from the router. See "'Signal Lost' Message on Your BlazzBox: What It Means."
