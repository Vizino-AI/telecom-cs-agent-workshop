# Forgot Your Router Admin Password?

**Article:** KB-15 · **Category:** Equipment & Network Settings · **Applies to:** BlazzRouter R100, R200

Your router admin password lets you into your router's settings page at 192.168.1.1. That's where you change your Wi-Fi name and password, set up a guest network, and use parental controls. It's different from your Wi-Fi password.

## First, check the sticker

If you've never changed your admin password, it's printed on the sticker on the bottom of your router, labeled "Admin Password." The sticker also shows your original Wi-Fi name and Wi-Fi password.

## If you changed it and forgot it

A changed admin password can't be looked up — not even by Blazz Support. The only way back in is a **factory reset**, which puts the router back to its original settings.

## What a factory reset erases

- Your Wi-Fi name and password (they go back to the ones on the sticker)
- Your admin password (goes back to the one on the sticker)
- Your guest network
- Your parental controls

After a reset, every device needs to reconnect using the Wi-Fi name and password on the sticker. That includes a BlazzBox X200 on Wi-Fi. BlazzMesh Extenders also need to be set up again using their setup card.

## How to do a factory reset

1. Make sure the router is plugged in and turned on.
2. Find the small pinhole labeled **Reset** on the back of the router.
3. Use a straightened paper clip to press and hold the button inside for **10 seconds**.
4. Let go. The router will restart, which takes up to 5 minutes.
5. When the light is solid green, go to 192.168.1.1 and log in with the admin password on the sticker.
6. Set up your Wi-Fi name, password, and any other settings again.

## Important

- A factory reset is only for a forgotten admin password. **Don't use it to fix a connection problem.** It won't fix a blinking amber or solid red light, and you'll lose your settings.
- If you only forgot your **Wi-Fi password**, you don't need a reset. Log in to the settings page with your admin password and look it up on the Wi-Fi page.

## Keep your passwords safe

Write down any new passwords and keep them somewhere safe at home.
