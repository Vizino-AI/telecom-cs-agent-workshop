# The Light Is Still Wrong After a Restart: What to Do Next

**Article:** KB-06 · **Category:** Status Lights & Troubleshooting · **Applies to:** BlazzRouter R100, R200 · BlazzBox X150, X200

You've restarted your BlazzRouter or BlazzBox once, but the problem is still there. Here's what to do next, based on what the light shows now. You don't need to restart again — one restart is enough.

## Solid green, but something still doesn't work

The device is connected to the Blazz network, so the problem is somewhere else:

- **Only one device can't get online?** The problem is that device. Restart it, or forget the Wi-Fi network on it and join again. See "Mobile Data Works but Home Wi-Fi Doesn't: What's Wrong?"
- **Internet is slow?** See "Slow Internet: Common Causes and What to Check."
- **Wi-Fi drops in some rooms?** See "Wi-Fi Keeps Cutting In and Out: Check Where Your Router Is."

## Blinking green

The device is installing a software update. Wait, and don't unplug it. Updates take up to 10 minutes. If it's still blinking green after 30 minutes, contact Blazz Support.

## Blinking amber

The device is still trying to connect. Give it time. If it's still blinking amber 30 minutes after your restart, contact Blazz Support. A technician visit may be needed.

## Solid red

There's a problem with the Blazz line or with the device. This can't be fixed at home. Contact Blazz Support to book a technician visit. See "When to Book a Technician Visit."

## No light

The device has no power. Check that the power cable is firmly plugged in at both ends, and try a different outlet. If there's still no light, the device or its power adapter may be faulty. Contact Blazz Support and we'll send you a replacement. See "When to Replace Equipment Instead of Troubleshooting."

## When you contact us

Tell Blazz Support:

- Which device it is (router or BlazzBox), and the model if you know it
- The light color, and whether it's solid or blinking
- That you've already restarted, and how many times
- When the problem started

That way, we won't ask you to repeat steps you've already tried.
